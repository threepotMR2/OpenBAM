These are two 3D printable moulds for making your own poly bushes to replace the front and rear engine mounts that resist the engine torque.

Remove your engine mounts, remove the rubber, clean up the original metal bolt tube, and remove the outer sleeve.

Simply print these two mould cups with your buddies 3D printer, then fill with the following resin options.

For hard mounts suitable for race/motorsport/track use, recommended resin is:- "DuroFlex 95 Polyurethane Liquid Casting Rubber 1kg Kit"

For more compliant mounts, recommended minimum hardess resin is:- "Polytek Poly PT Flex 70 Liquid Casting Rubber 1kg kit" This give a nicely compliant pair of mount.

1kg will make approminately 3 pairs of mounts.
